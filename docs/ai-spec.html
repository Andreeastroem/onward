AI Specification (v1 baseline)

Status
v1 baseline
Area
Systems

Purpose #

Defines the measurable behavior of skirmish AI so it can serve playtesting and ship as a product feature. It resolves item 6 of the design review. Economy rules come from Economy design, theft and bank rules from Resource ownership, and the micro ceiling from Combat.

The AI is one shared decision engine. Tiers and profiles are parameter sets on that engine, not separate implementations.

Difficulty Tiers #

v1 ships three tiers: Beginner, Standard, Hard. Hard has no hidden resource or vision bonus. The response delay applies between valid information becoming available and the AI's first responsive order; it does not apply to simulation or pathing.

TierResponse delayMax avg production idleMax unspent biomassMin raid cooldown
Beginner0.75 s35%45%150 s
Standard0.40 s20%30%90 s
Hard0.20 s10%20%60 s

Macro Quality KPIs #

  • Primary KPIs: average production-source idle time, and unspent spendable biomass as a share of recent income.
  • Both are measured over a rolling 60 s window.
  • Biomass explicitly reserved for an active, visible tech or production purchase is excluded from unspent biomass.
  • Worker-count milestones are optional diagnostics that may vary by profile and build order. They are not pass/fail gates.

Route Raids #

A raid requires both a cooldown and an opportunity gate. The cooldown is measured from the previous raid's completion or abort (see tier table). The opportunity gate requires scouted exposed logistics and acceptable local force odds. Difficulty changes cadence and risk tolerance; fixed raid schedules alone are prohibited.

Strategic Profiles #

Four curated profiles exist: Economic, Aggressive, Balanced, Raiding. Each is a weighted parameter set over five axes on the shared engine. All tiers may switch freely among profiles during a match. Profiles are reassessed every 30 s and retained for at least 60 s. Only a core threat, the upkeep-stress trigger, or a validated exploit response may override the commitment window.

Profile values are relative bands, not locked numbers. Exact weights are tuned within the stated ordering without a spec change.

Upkeep Stress Recovery #

Trigger: the AI projects net-negative biomass over 20 s and its spendable reserve covers under 30 s of projected upkeep. While triggered the AI:

  1. pauses optional tech and high-upkeep queues;
  2. cancels only unstarted nonessential queue items;
  3. prioritizes safe deposits and low-upkeep defense;
  4. never auto-disbands existing units.

Deficit Recovery #

Measured in a fixed fair scenario that starts at a 20% army-value deficit.

TierRequirementTime limit
BeginnerStabilize: deficit grows no more than 10% further90 s
StandardReduce gap to 10% or less4 min
HardReach 5% gap or better in at least 70% of regression runs4 min

Exploit Counters #

Release-blocking: AI v1 must detect and counter all four classes.

  1. Repeated bait-and-retreat loops.
  2. Unescorted carrier or relay raid loops (see Resource ownership).
  3. Static choke or Weaver-anchor-exit camping that causes unit feeding.
  4. Unreachable or invalid pathing and placement exploitation.

Regression Testing #

  • Release gate: a fixed, seeded scenario suite run per tier. It covers the macro KPIs, raid gating, upkeep stress, deficit recovery, and each exploit class. Every KPI above must pass per tier.
  • Balance report: a periodic AI-vs-AI ladder across tiers and profiles. It reports win rates and is informational, not a release gate.

Decision Trace #

Every AI regression match emits a compact full trace containing:

  • observed inputs and visibility;
  • active profile and switch reason;
  • response-delay timestamps;
  • production-idle and resource-float samples;
  • raid gate and cooldown decisions;
  • upkeep-stress state;
  • exploit-detection responses.

Open Items #

  • Scenario list, seed count per scenario, and the ladder match count are not yet defined.
  • Detection heuristics for each exploit class are not yet specified.
  • Confirm the qualitative profile orderings in the profiles table.
